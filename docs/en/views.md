# Views and Navigation

> **Version:** v0.2 (beta)

MindMapper offers two layout modes — **Hierarchical** and **Radial** — plus controls to pan and zoom freely around your map.

---

## Switching Layouts

Use the two buttons in the middle of the toolbar to switch between views:

| Button label | Layout |
|---|---|
| **Jerárquica** | Hierarchical — tree flows from top to bottom |
| **Radial** | Radial — nodes radiate outward from the centre |

> **Note:** The layout buttons currently display in Spanish ("Jerárquica", "Radial") regardless of the app language setting. This will be corrected in a future release.

[SCREENSHOT: The two layout buttons highlighted in the toolbar]

---

## Hierarchical Layout (Default)

The Hierarchical layout organises your map as a tree flowing **top to bottom**:

- The **root node** appears at the top centre.
- **Child nodes** spread downward, level by level.
- Each level is arranged horizontally so it is easy to read at a glance.

This is the recommended layout for outlines, project plans, and anything with clear parent–child relationships.

[SCREENSHOT: A mind map displayed in Hierarchical layout]

---

## Radial Layout

The Radial layout places the **root node at the centre**, with branches radiating outward in all directions like spokes on a wheel.

- Works best for **brainstorming** and free exploration of ideas.
- All branches get equal visual weight.

> ⚠️ **Note (v0.2):** The Radial layout is functional. Visual spacing improvements are planned for a future release.

## Panning (Moving Around the Canvas)

To scroll through a large map, **click and drag** on any empty area of the canvas. You can pan in any direction without limits.

---

## Zooming

| Action | Keyboard shortcut | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Zoom in | **Ctrl + +** | Toolbar **+** button, or mouse wheel up |
| Zoom out | **Ctrl + −** | Toolbar **−** button, or mouse wheel down |
| Reset to 100 % | **Ctrl + 0** | **Reset View** button (bottom-right of canvas) |
| Fit all nodes on screen | **Ctrl + 1** | **Fit to Screen** button (bottom-right of canvas) |

The current zoom level is shown as a percentage in the toolbar (between the **−** and **+** buttons) and also in the bottom-right corner of the canvas.

[SCREENSHOT: Zoom percentage in the toolbar and the canvas overlay buttons in the bottom-right corner]

## Canvas Controls (Bottom-Right Corner)

Two icon buttons are always visible in the bottom-right corner of the canvas:

| Icon | Button name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| ⤢ (Maximize2) | **Fit to Screen** | Zooms and pans so every node is visible at once |
| ↺ (RotateCcw) | **Reset View** | Returns to 100 % zoom, centred |

[SCREENSHOT: The Fit to Screen and Reset View buttons in the bottom-right corner]

---

## Fullscreen Mode

Press **F11** to toggle fullscreen. This hides the operating system window frame and gives your map the entire screen.
